What we do

SISL works on the edge of internet-facing services: the layer that every online banking session, payment, patient-portal login and partner connection passes through.

Focus areas

  • Bot and abuse mitigation

    Limiting automated attacks such as credential stuffing and account takeover.

  • Attack-resilient load balancing

    Keeping services available during traffic floods, provider outages and failed changes.

  • Real-time traffic analytics

    Showing operators what is reaching their services, so problems are found early.

  • Integrity of edge software and third-party code

    Knowing which components and scripts run at the edge, and detecting unauthorized changes.

How we work

  • Engineering services

    For regulated institutions and the software vendors that serve them.

  • Open-source software

    Tools any organization can use.

  • Public research

    Free to use, including by institutions that are not clients.

Who we work with

Community and regional banks, credit unions and fintechs; healthcare software vendors; and regional health systems and rural hospitals.
