About SISL

Sovereign Infrastructure Software Lab, LLC is an engineering company founded in November 2026 in Ashburn, Virginia, to improve the security and resilience of the internet-facing services of U.S. financial and healthcare institutions.

Founder

Mykhailo (Mike) Stefantsiv

Founder and Chief Executive Officer

Mike has more than ten years of experience in software engineering and in building and leading engineering teams. He is currently an engineering lead at HubSpot, Inc., where he leads the teams responsible for its edge and load-balancing infrastructure. SISL is independent of HubSpot and is not affiliated with it. He previously led an engineering team at a major U.S. bank that rebuilt core transaction-processing systems on cloud infrastructure, and earlier worked on healthcare data integration.

He holds bachelor's and master's degrees in software engineering from Lviv Polytechnic National University in Ukraine.
