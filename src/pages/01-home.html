Edge security and traffic resilience for U.S. financial-services and healthcare platforms

Sovereign Infrastructure Software Lab, LLC (SISL) helps banks, credit unions and healthcare organizations keep their online services secure and available. Our work covers bot and abuse mitigation, attack-resilient load balancing and real-time traffic analytics, including the integrity of the third-party software and services these systems depend on.

What we do

  • Engineering services

    Helping regulated institutions and their software vendors strengthen the internet-facing layer of their services.

  • Open-source software

    Open-source tools for traffic analytics and bot detection.

  • Public research

    Free, openly licensed guidance written for smaller institutions.

Why it matters

Financial services and healthcare are U.S. critical infrastructure. Their online services depend on shared providers and third-party software, so one failure can reach many institutions at once. Smaller institutions face the same risks with far fewer staff.
