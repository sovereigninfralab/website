Privacy notice

Sovereign Infrastructure Software Lab, LLC ("SISL") operates this website. Last updated November 26, 2026.

What this website collects

This website has no forms, no accounts, no advertising and no third-party tracking. It does not set cookies and does not run scripts in your browser. Its fonts and images are served from this site, not from other providers.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information about each request, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Cloudflare handles that information under its own privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

If you email us

If you write to an address on this site, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and the content of your message. We use them only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. We keep business correspondence for as long as it is needed for that purpose or required by law. We do not sell or share it for marketing.

Security reports are handled as described in SISL's security policy.

Links to other sites

This site links to other websites, such as GitHub and government sources. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Changes and contact

If this notice changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: info@sovereigninfralab.com.
